What sponsorship makes possible

Maintaining Sokrates takes time. Your support helps sustain the work behind the tool:

  • Development and maintenance: improving the analysis, fixing bugs, and preparing releases.
  • Testing and compatibility: keeping Sokrates useful across languages, platforms, and evolving codebases.
  • Documentation and examples: helping developers, architects, and engineering teams get more from their reports.

Free and open source for everyone

Sokrates is available under the MIT license. Individuals, teams, and commercial organizations can all use it.

Sokrates will remain free and open source. Sponsorship is entirely voluntary.

Contributions go to Željko Obrenović through his GitHub Sponsors profile and support his open-source work, including Sokrates.
